      How do I get an ENEX file?

      In Evernote for Windows or Mac, right-click a notebook, select Export, and choose ENEX. Include note attributes such as tags and dates. Evernote Web and the iPhone app do not provide this notebook export workflow.

      Each file’s notebook name applies to all notes in that file. Export one notebook at a time when you want to preserve those groupings.

      Connect your iPhone and this computer to the same trusted Wi-Fi. Keep this app running on the computer, then open its private link in Safari.

      Local-network sharing is off. Start the app with its phone-sharing launcher to get a private link.

      Anyone with this link on your network can access the archive. Share it only with people you trust.

      Return notes to Evernote

      Choose Take this notebook → Evernote ENEX. In Evernote for Windows or Mac, use File → Import and choose that file. After syncing, check the notes in Evernote on your iPhone.

      This restores the original export. Notebook organization may need to be arranged in Evernote. The custom archive ZIP is for storage, not direct Evernote import.
